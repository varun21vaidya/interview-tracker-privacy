# Interview Tracker

**Keep your whole job search in one place. Private by design.**

Interview Tracker is a free Android app for tracking job interviews. Add a company, follow each interview round from the first recruiter call to the final result, and always know what is next, who you are waiting on, and when to follow up.

## What it does

- **Finds interview invitations** in the calendars you choose and files them under the right company. Anything unclear goes to a short "Needs review" list for you to decide.
- **Shows what is next:** your next interview, the rounds still waiting for a result, and when you last followed up.
- **Reminds you** before interviews you add by hand.
- **Writes a polite follow-up email** with the company, role and round filled in, and opens it in your own mail app.
- **Keeps recruiter contacts, notes and the job description** beside each company.

## Private by design

- Your data stays on your phone. There is no account with us, no server and no ads.
- Sign in with Google to back up to your own Google Drive, in a hidden folder only this app can use. Restore it on a new phone with one tap.
- The calendar is read only. The app never changes it.
- The developer never receives your data.

## Why the app asks for Google access

Interview Tracker uses Google Sign-In only to save and restore your backups in the private app folder of your own Google Drive (the `drive.appdata` scope) and to show which account is signed in. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
