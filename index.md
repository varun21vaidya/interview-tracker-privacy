# Privacy Policy for Interview Tracker

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Interview Tracker ("the app") is an Android app that helps you track job interviews. This page explains what the app reads, what it stores, and where that data goes. The short version: **your data stays on your phone and in your own Google Drive. The developer has no server and never receives your data.**

## What the app reads

- **Your calendar (read only).** After you allow it, the app reads events from the calendars **you choose** in Settings, to find interview invitations. It reads the title, time, location, notes, meeting link, phone number and organizer email of those events. The app never changes your calendar.
- **Your Google name and email.** If you sign in with Google, the app receives your name and email address so it can show who is signed in and, if you want, put your name in emails you write.

## What the app stores

- **On your phone:** the companies, interview rounds, recruiter contacts, notes, job descriptions, your email signature and the settings you enter. This is kept in the app's private storage.
- **In your own Google Drive:** if you sign in and leave automatic backup on, the app saves backup files of the data above in a hidden folder that belongs only to this app (the Google Drive "app data folder"). The app cannot see or change any other file in your Drive, and you cannot see this folder in the normal Drive view. You can delete these backups at any time from the app (Settings, Google Drive, Delete all Drive backups).
- **How backups are protected:** they are sent over HTTPS and stored in your own Google Drive, protected by your Google account. Anyone who can sign in to your Google account could restore them.

## What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no developer server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. Data moves only between your phone, your calendar and your own Google Drive.

## Notifications

Reminders for interviews you add by hand are created on your phone. They do not use a server.

## Email follow-ups

The app can prepare an email and open your own mail app with the text filled in. The app does not send email itself.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Read calendar | Find interview invitations in the calendars you choose |
| Notifications | Remind you of interviews you add by hand |
| Internet | Sign in with Google and save or restore Drive backups |

## Your choices and deleting your data

- **Stop reading a calendar:** Settings, Calendars to read, Remove.
- **Delete Drive backups:** Settings, Google Drive, Delete all Drive backups.
- **Sign out:** the app first saves a final backup (if needed), then removes your interviews, name and signature from the phone.
- **Remove everything:** delete the Drive backups, sign out, then uninstall the app.
- **Revoke access:** open your Google Account, Security, Third-party access, and remove Interview Tracker.

## Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and it does not knowingly handle their data.
